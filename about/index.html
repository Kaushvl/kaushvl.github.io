// 01 — bio

I'm Kaushal — an ML engineer with around two years of building production AI systems. I'm currently a data scientist at NexusLink Services, where I work across LLM applications, RAG, and audio ML.

Most of what I do sits between research and infrastructure. I like the part of the job where a model finally meets real users, with all the latency, cost and reliability constraints that come with it. I write a lot of FastAPI, a lot of evaluation harnesses, and a healthy amount of glue code.

Before this I interned at RiverEdge Analytics, where I built a multimodal invoice-extraction pipeline. I've also shipped a handful of personal projects — they're on the work page.

Outside of work I solve LeetCode for fun (530-ish so far), read more ML papers than I can keep up with, and tinker with on-device inference.

// 04 — how i work

A few opinions, kept short.

  • Ship measurable impact, not demos. A working notebook is a third of the job.
  • Production from day one. Versioning, monitoring, fallbacks — built in, not bolted on.
  • Smallest model that meets the bar. Cost and latency are first-class metrics, not afterthoughts.
  • Prompts and evals are code. They get reviewed, versioned and tested like everything else.
  • Write things down. Future-me is the most common reader of any system I build.
